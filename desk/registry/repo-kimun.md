# Repository: kimun

## Description

_Describe the repository purpose, scope, and role in the ecosystem._

Kimun is the v2 data/document layer (decision 17): a stable store (anchors and hashes of the
environment; format 4 keeps document trees in a recomputable cache) plus the Markdown, models and
query surfaces. `sldb` v1 and `kgdb` are frozen and superseded by it.
