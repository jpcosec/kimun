# W_i context node and index

## Answer

W_i (a Matrix logical context) is both a G-class node of kind :context ({:name}) and a tree-index of kind :context over the pool whose children are the admissible terms and relations. Binding (S to M) and projection (M to G) edges name their W_i in evidence; the same sign may bind to different symbols in different W_i, one edge per context. In milestones 0-3 a W_i is only a node plus a tree; no matrix evaluation.
