# Re-anchoring rules under succession

## Answer

When X' supersedes X: ownership edges are rewritten by the replacing transaction itself; reference and binding edges follow the successor automatically (a new edge to X' with fresh evidence; the old one stays as superseded, never deleted); semantic edges are not followed and stay superseded for human or agent review because meaning may have changed; projection edges stay superseded pending revalidation of the fact's sense status; derived edges are invalidated and recomputed on demand; supersedes edges chain. For an external change (drifted) heuristic reconciliation proposes X' with a confidence; once accepted, supersedes is recorded and the same rules apply.
