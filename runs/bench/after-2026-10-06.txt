Per commit ~13 KB is the log line and ~14 KB the resolved plan (it carries the document
text); the rest are new nodes, tree objects and mmap nodes. The slow rise of bytes and
objects per commit is the depth of the hash tries (log16 of the map sizes), not history.
Before (baseline, format 1): 56 ms/commit at 200 docs and 95 ms at 400, growing linearly;
16.4 MB at 200 docs, 51.2 MB at 400.
